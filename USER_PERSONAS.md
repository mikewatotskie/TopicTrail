# TopicTrail: Provisional User Personas

**Team JAM · C-6 | SDG 4: Quality Education**

> **Evidence status:** These personas are hypothetical design tools. They are not based on confirmed interviews or real participant quotations. The team should validate or revise them through student research as required by the ITMSD1 Week 1 checkpoint.

## Primary persona: A student who studies from a phone

| Attribute | Provisional profile |
| --- | --- |
| Persona label | **Student A: The Topic Explorer** |
| Context | College student who regularly receives digital course syllabi |
| Main device | Android smartphone |
| Goal | Understand unfamiliar topics before or after class without paying for additional materials |
| Study habit | Reopens a syllabus to check which concepts to review |
| Possible pain points | Frequently switching apps, ambiguous search terms, long lists of unrelated web results, difficulty keeping useful links |
| Needs | Selectable syllabus text, quick study action, clear resource titles, save-for-later option |
| Success scenario | Selects a topic in a syllabus, finds a relevant free lesson, and bookmarks it |

### Empathy-map hypotheses

- **Thinks:** “I know the topic name, but where should I start?”
- **Feels:** Unsure which resource is useful when many links appear.
- **Does:** Opens a syllabus, switches to a browser, searches manually, and returns to the file.
- **Needs:** A connected reading-to-study flow and understandable source information.

*These are illustrative statements, not direct student quotes.*

### TopicTrail opportunity

Make the selected phrase the starting point for a clearly explained resource recommendation workflow.

## Secondary persona: A student who organizes study materials

| Attribute | Provisional profile |
| --- | --- |
| Persona label | **Student B: The Organized Reviewer** |
| Context | College student managing multiple subjects and syllabus documents |
| Main device | Android phone, occasionally a laptop |
| Goal | Keep course topics and free study links organized for upcoming activities and assessments |
| Study habit | Revisits the same resources across several study sessions |
| Possible pain points | Misplacing links, forgetting why a resource was saved, difficulty finding the original syllabus topic |
| Needs | Document library, topic-linked bookmarks, visible recent items, persistent preferences |
| Success scenario | Reopens a syllabus and quickly returns to a previously saved resource |

### Empathy-map hypotheses

- **Thinks:** “I already found a useful explanation; I want to find it again.”
- **Feels:** Frustrated when helpful links are scattered across apps.
- **Does:** Saves browser tabs, takes notes, or manually organizes links.
- **Needs:** Local document organization and topic-aware bookmarks.

*These are illustrative statements, not direct student quotes.*

### TopicTrail opportunity

Provide a syllabus library and saved-resource area that minimize repeated searches.

## Questions for validation

1. How do students currently receive and study their course syllabi?
2. What do they do when they encounter a topic they do not understand?
3. How do they decide whether a learning resource is trustworthy and useful?
4. What problems arise when switching between the syllabus and a browser?
5. Would Highlight-to-Study and saved resources improve their workflow?
6. What accessibility or connectivity barriers do they encounter?
