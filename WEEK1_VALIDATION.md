## Week 1 goal

Define a specific student-centered learning problem, describe intended users, map the mobile information architecture and user journey, and prepare low-fidelity wireframes in Figma/FigJam.

## Working problem and solution

**Problem:** Students studying from digital syllabi may need to switch between their document and unrelated searches to understand unfamiliar topics and locate helpful free resources.

**How Might We:** How might we help students using digital course syllabi turn a selected topic into a short list of relevant, free learning resources without repeatedly switching between unrelated apps?

**Proposed solution:** TopicTrail connects text selection in a syllabus to a **Study** action, educational resource recommendations, basic link screening, in-app viewing, and bookmarks.

Read [Problem Statement](docs/PROBLEM_STATEMENT.md), [User Personas](USER_PERSONAS.md), and [User Flow](USER_FLOW.md).
